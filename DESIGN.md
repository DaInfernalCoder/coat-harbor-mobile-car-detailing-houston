# Coat Harbor design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want exterior and interior care brought into one settled service plan. The page should make that plan clear and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Harbor ink — `#102A33`
- Deep water — `#1E4650`
- Channel blue — `#398EA1`
- Dock cream — `#F4F1E8`
- Coat copper — `#D8773B`
- Display: Barlow Condensed
- Body: Manrope

## Layout thesis

The route frame becomes a harbor approach: assess condition, bring each surface into scope, schedule, and settle the final review.

## Signature

A coated arch rests over two controlled waterlines, expressing a stable finish without marine scenery or nautical clichés.

## Critique

The first concept leaned on anchors and waves. The revision removes literal nautical objects and keeps one quiet harbor geometry.
